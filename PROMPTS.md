# Game Asset Generation Prompts

This document provides detailed, high-quality prompts for generating consistent 2D game assets using Text-to-Image AI (like Midjourney, DALL-E 3, or Stable Diffusion).

## Style Guide
**Core Aesthetic:** Clean Pixel Art, 16-bit / 32-bit style, vibrant colors, soft lighting, 3/4 top-down perspective (RPG Maker style).
**Background:** Always use "pure white background" or "transparent background" to make extraction easier.
**Technical Details:** 16x16 or 32x32 sprite size per frame, consistent line thickness, no motion blur.

---

## 1. Main Player Character
> **Prompt:** Professional pixel art spritesheet of a survivalist hero character, 3/4 top-down perspective, 16-bit style. Male, brown messy hair, blue shirt, dark trousers. Spritesheet includes 4-direction walk cycle (Down, Left, Right, Up) and idle pose. Each sprite framed in a 32x32 grid. Vibrant colors, clean outlines, high contrast, pure white background.

## 2. Enemies (Skeleton / Zombie)
> **Prompt:** Professional pixel art spritesheet of a fantasy skeleton warrior, 3/4 top-down perspective, 16-bit style. Empty glowing red eye sockets, tattered grey rags, whitened bones. Spritesheet includes 4-direction walk cycle (shambling movement) and idle pose. Each sprite framed in a 32x32 grid. Moody lighting, clean outlines, pure white background.

## 3. Passive Animals (Sheep, Pig, Chicken)
> **Sheep:** Pixel art spritesheet of a cute fluffy white sheep, top-down perspective. 4-direction walk cycle. 16-bit style, vibrant grassy colors, pure white background.
> **Pig:** Pixel art spritesheet of a small pink piglet, top-down perspective. 4-direction walk cycle. Clean pixel lines, soft shading, pure white background.
> **Chicken:** Pixel art spritesheet of a small brown hen/chicken, top-down perspective. 4-direction walking and pecking animation. 16-bit style, pure white background.

## 4. Slime Monster
> **Prompt:** Pixel art spritesheet of a bouncy green translucent slime monster, top-down perspective. 4-direction jump/walk cycle. Liquid jelly texture, inner glow, expressive eyes. 16-bit style, pure white background.

## 5. Environmental Resources

> **Rocks/Stone:** Collection of grey granite rocks and boulders, top-down perspective. Sharp edges, varied shapes, subtle moss details. Pixel art style, 32x32 size, white background.
